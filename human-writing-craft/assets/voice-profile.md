# Voice profile template

Build from the writer's own representative samples or an authorized style guide. Describe behavior, not a fictional personality.

```markdown
# Voice profile: [name or context]

## Scope
- Language:
- Genres covered:
- Audience:
- Samples reviewed:
- Confidence / gaps:

## Baseline habits
- Openings:
- Claim and evidence order:
- Typical sentence and paragraph range:
- Transitions:
- Vocabulary and terminology:
- Certainty and qualification:
- First person / direct address:
- Humor and emotion:
- Punctuation and formatting:
- Endings:

## Occasional signatures
- [Trait, function, approximate frequency]

## Avoid
- [Patterns absent from or inconsistent with the samples]

## Protected quirks
- [Harmless habits that should not be “corrected”]

## Demonstrated edits
| Generic version | Voice-consistent version | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... |
```

Do not copy distinctive sample phrases into the profile. Do not turn occasional features into quotas.
